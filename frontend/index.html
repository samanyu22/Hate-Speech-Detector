Preprocessing Studio

          
Inspect the actual transformation stages for text, multilingual code-mixing, memes, and context.


          

            

              
Text & Script Normalization

              
Applies Unicode NFKC normalization, URL neutralization ([URL]), mention anonymization ([MENTION]), camelCase hashtag decomposition, and Devanagari script integrity checking.

              
Raw: "Inko maro! #DeshBhakti https://t.co/xyz"
Normalized: "Inko maro ! Desh Bhakti [URL]"

            

            

              
Code-Mixed Hinglish Detection

              
Detects Latin vs Devanagari unicode characters and matches phonetic Romanized Hindi lexicon (e.g. kamine, bhai, desh, aukaat) to reliably identify Hinglish code-mixing without English translation loss.

            

          


          

            

              
Visual & EasyOCR Extraction

              
Loads meme graphics, resizes to [224, 224], extracts embedded text and spatial bounding boxes via EasyOCR, and normalizes pixel tensors for cross-modal cross-attention.

            

            

              
Context Sanitization

              
Sanitizes thread history and masks protected demographic references ([DEMOGRAPHIC_NEUTRAL]) to prevent models from learning identity-term biases.

Explainability (XAI) Studio

          
Grounded token-level attribution, visual bounding saliency, and AOPC faithfulness validation.


          

            
Methodology: Grounded Perturbation & Integrated Gradients

            
Traditional attention maps frequently fail faithfulness tests. Our engine implements Leave-One-Out (LIME-style) perturbation attribution and verifies explanation faithfulness by measuring confidence drop (Δf(x)) when top-k attributed tokens are ablated.

            
Faithfulness Score Formula: AOPC_Drop = |f(x) - f(x \ top_k)| / f(x)

Equalized Odds Mathematical Formulation

              

                A binary classifier satisfies Equalized Odds with respect to protected attribute A and ground truth Y if the prediction Ŷ is conditionally independent of A given Y:
              

              
P(Ŷ = 1 | Y = y, A = a) = P(Ŷ = 1 | Y = y, A = b) ∀ y ∈ {0, 1}

              

                Our platform enforces Equalized Odds Disparity Δ ≤ 0.15 across Hindi, English, and Hinglish code-mixed streams.
              

            


            

              
Protected Identity Neutralization Safeguards

              

                Identities (religions, castes, nationalities) are safeguarded against false-positive bias. Mentioning a protected demographic in a benign or positive context never triggers a hate label without explicit hostile or exclusionary predicates.
